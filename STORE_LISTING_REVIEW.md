## 1. Store Listing Policy Audit

Google Play Metadata Policy prohibits:
- Unsubstantiated superlative claims ("#1 app", "best PDF tool", "100% fastest").
- Deceptive offline claims ("100% offline" when AdMob or Billing requires internet).
- Misleading pricing claims ("Free forever" when subscriptions exist).
- Keyword stuffing or spammy emoji repetitions in title or short description.
