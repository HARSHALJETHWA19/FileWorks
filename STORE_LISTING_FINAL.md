```text
FileWorks is your all-in-one local file utility suite designed for speed, simplicity, and complete privacy. All document operations and transformations happen directly on your device using local processing isolates.

CORE FEATURES:

📄 COMPLETE PDF TOOLKIT
• Merge PDFs: Combine multiple PDF documents into a single organized file with drag-and-drop page reordering.
• Split PDF: Extract specific pages, odd/even ranges, or break multi-page documents into separate files.
• Rotate PDF: Correct the orientation of individual pages or entire documents (90°, 180°, 270°).
• Reorder PDF: Rearrange pages visually in your preferred reading sequence.
• PDF to Image: Convert PDF pages into high-resolution JPG or PNG images.
• Image to PDF: Assemble photos, receipts, and scanned documents into clean, compact PDFs.
• Compress PDF: Reduce document file size with customizable Low, Medium, and High compression presets while maintaining text and visual clarity.

🖼️ POWERFUL IMAGE TOOLS
• Image Compress: Optimize image sizes with custom target dimensions and compression quality presets.
• Image Resize: Scale width and height with aspect ratio locking and smooth pixel scaling.
• Image Convert: Convert seamlessly between JPG, PNG, and WebP formats on-device.

📦 ARCHIVE & BATCH UTILITIES
• Create ZIP: Bundle multiple documents, photos, and files into secure compressed ZIP archives.
• Extract ZIP: Unpack archives with built-in path-traversal protection. Save all extracted files individually to your storage or repackage as a clean ZIP.
• Batch Rename: Format, prefix, suffix, and sequentially renumber groups of files in bulk.

🔒 LOCAL-FIRST PRIVACY PROMISE
• On-Device Processing: All file transformations execute locally on your device's processor.
• Zero Cloud Exfiltration: FileWorks does not operate cloud storage servers and does not upload your files, images, or documents to any remote server.
• No Account Required: Use all core features without registration, accounts, or invitations.
• Safe Temporary Storage: Working files generated during processing are kept in your device's private app sandbox and cleared automatically.
```
