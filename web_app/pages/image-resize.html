Frequently Asked Questions

Will resizing stretch or distort my photo?
No, as long as "Lock aspect ratio" remains checked, adjusting the width automatically recalculates the height proportionally, avoiding any distortion.
Can I enlarge small images?
Yes, you can scale images up. However, enlarging raster images beyond their native resolution will inevitably decrease sharpness.
Are images uploaded anywhere?
No. FileWorks executes all image scaling directly on your computer using high-quality bicubic canvas algorithms.
