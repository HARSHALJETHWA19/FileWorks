Frequently Asked Questions

What resolution are the converted images?
Pages are rendered at 2x scale (approximately 144 DPI) for crisp readability of text and graphics.
Should I pick JPG or PNG?
Choose JPG for standard documents and photos to keep download file sizes small. Choose PNG for charts, line art, or documents where maximum sharpness is needed.
Are my PDF documents uploaded?
No. FileWorks uses Mozilla's PDF.js library to render pages directly inside your browser. No files are transferred to any server.
