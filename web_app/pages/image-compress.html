Frequently Asked Questions

What is the free limit for image compression?
The free tier allows compressing images up to 10 MB total input size across all selected files.
How is the total input size calculated?
The file sizes of all selected images are summed together. If the combined total exceeds 10 MB, the free limit is reached.
Are images uploaded to any server?
No. Images are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to FileWorks servers.
