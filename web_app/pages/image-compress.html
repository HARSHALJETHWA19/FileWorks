Frequently Asked Questions

How does browser-based image compression work?
FileWorks utilizes your browser's native HTML5 Canvas API and modern image codecs. It optimizes the image data locally without needing to transmit anything over the internet.
Will my compressed images lose clarity?
At the recommended 82% quality setting, file sizes drop significantly (often 40-70%) while remaining virtually indistinguishable from the original to the human eye.
Why convert to WebP?
WebP provides superior lossless and lossy compression for web images, often saving 25-35% more space than JPEG at equivalent quality.
