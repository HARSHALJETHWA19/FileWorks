Frequently Asked Questions

What is the free limit for PDF rotation?
The free tier allows rotating PDFs with up to 5 pages per operation, matching the Android application.
Does rotating a PDF degrade quality?
No. The internal PDF rotation flag is adjusted using pdf-lib without re-rasterizing pages, preserving 100% of the vector text and graphics.
Is processing private?
Yes, all rotations happen locally in your browser without transmitting your files across any network.
