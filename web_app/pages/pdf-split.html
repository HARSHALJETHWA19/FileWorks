Frequently Asked Questions

What is the free limit for splitting PDFs?
The free tier allows extracting up to 5 pages or 5 selections per operation.
Are extracted files re-compressed or modified?
No. All original vectors, fonts, resolutions, and formatting are completely preserved by pdf-lib.
Can I download individual pages?
Yes, every split output has its own Preview and Download button, and there is also a Download All as ZIP button.
