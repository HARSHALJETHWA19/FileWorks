Technical Specifications & Extraction Rules

When extracting pages from a PDF, FileWorks traverses the document's /Pages catalog and copies only the fonts, color profiles, and image dictionaries referenced by the extracted pages:

Frequently Asked Questions

Are physical page numbers the same as printed page numbers?
PDF tools operate on physical sheet sequence (where page 1 is the front cover). If a book or report has introductory Roman numerals (e.g. pages i–iv) before chapter 1, specify physical sheet indices rather than the printed page number in the footer.
Will extracted pages lose font formatting or image quality?
No. FileWorks extracts the raw vector drawing instructions and embedded font dictionaries without re-encoding or rasterizing. Every extracted page is 100% identical in quality to the original.
What is the free limit for splitting?
The free tier supports extracting up to 5 pages/selections per operation. You can process consecutive batches without limits or payment walls.
