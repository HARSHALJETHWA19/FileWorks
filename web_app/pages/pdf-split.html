Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extract multiple pages at once?
Yes. Enter page numbers separated by commas (e.g. 1, 3, 5) or use ranges (e.g. 1-5, 10-15).
Are my files uploaded anywhere?
No. All splitting happens locally in your browser. Files are not sent to any server.
What formats does the output come in?
Each split part is a standard PDF file. When multiple files are produced, you can download them together as a ZIP archive.
