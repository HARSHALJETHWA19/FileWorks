Frequently Asked Questions

Is FileWorks PDF Merge completely free?
Yes, FileWorks is 100% free and ads-supported. There are no subscriptions, payment walls, or premium upgrades.
Are my PDF files uploaded to FileWorks servers?
No. Your files are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to FileWorks servers. All page copying and rendering happens in local browser memory.
What is the free tier limit for PDF merging?
The free tier allows merging up to 3 PDF files per operation, matching the Android application free limits.
Can I preview the merged file before saving?
Yes! Click the Preview button on the result screen to inspect your document directly in your browser.
