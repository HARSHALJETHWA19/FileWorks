About This Tool

📁 Supported Formats

PDF files (.pdf). All files must be valid, non-encrypted PDFs.

⚡ Limitations

Maximum 50 PDFs and 200 MB per file. Very large files may take longer to process depending on your device.

🔒 Privacy

Files are processed in your browser using pdf-lib. They are never uploaded to any server.

💾 Download

The merged PDF downloads directly from your browser memory to your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF merger free to use?
Yes, FileWorks PDF Merge is completely free. No subscription, no sign-up, no limits on personal use.
Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?
No. All merging happens in your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library. Your files never leave your device during processing.
Can I merge password-protected PDFs?
Currently, password-protected (encrypted) PDFs cannot be merged. Please remove the password protection first using your PDF viewer.
Will the merged PDF look exactly the same?
Yes. Page content, dimensions, and orientation are preserved. pdf-lib is a well-tested library used by millions of applications.
How many PDFs can I merge at once?
Up to 50 PDF files at once. Each file can be up to 200 MB. For very large batches, processing depends on your device's available memory.
