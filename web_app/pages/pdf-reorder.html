Frequently Asked Questions

Is reordering PDF pages secure?
Yes! The PDF is rearranged entirely in your web browser using WebAssembly and client-side JavaScript. Your file is never uploaded to any remote server.
Can I duplicate or delete pages?
To delete or extract specific pages, use our PDF Split tool. This tool specifically reorders the existing pages.
Is there a page count limit?
You can reorder documents with hundreds of pages smoothly. Performance depends on your device's memory.
