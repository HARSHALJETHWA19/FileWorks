Frequently Asked Questions

What is the free limit for PDF reordering?
The free tier allows reorganizing documents with up to 10 pages per operation, matching the Android application free limits.
Are page contents modified or cropped?
No. Internal page geometry, dimensions, fonts, and contents are preserved 100% without modification.
Are my files sent to any server?
No. All reordering runs client-side using JavaScript and pdf-lib in your device browser.
