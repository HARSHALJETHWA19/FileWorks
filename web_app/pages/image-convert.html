Frequently Asked Questions

What is the advantage of WebP?
WebP is a modern format developed by Google that offers 25-35% better compression compared to PNG and JPEG without perceptible loss in visual quality.
Does PNG keep transparent backgrounds?
Yes, converting from formats that support transparency (like PNG or WebP) to PNG will preserve transparent backgrounds. Converting to JPG will replace transparent pixels with a solid white background.
Is there any file upload limit?
Because conversion takes place right on your device, there are no internet upload wait times or server storage restrictions.
